There are two ways to learn: from history and from experience. Either we read the lessons that people have learned in the past and incorporate them into our corpus, or we take action and reflect on the outcome. 

The first option is easy to do (assuming we know how to [read](https://www.amazon.com/How-Read-Book-Classic-Intelligent/dp/0671212095)). It usually takes less resources, less time, can be done in comfort etc. But you have to know *what* to read, which can be hard to figure out with all the noise out there this day and age. 

The second option is hard to do. It takes considerably more effort and resources, and more direct consequences. However, the things we learn grow exponentially as a result of these consequences. 

The first option is low-risk/uncertain-reward and the second option is high-risk/higher-than-otherwise-reward. The question that remains is: what's your risk tolerance?
