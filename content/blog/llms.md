If it's not clear already - LLMs are the newest layer of abstraction in computer programming. We have now pushed high-level programming languages further down the software engineering stack. What this means is that we now have a new universe of bugs to discover and understand.

Unfortunately, we won't be able to discover *all* new bugs because of English!!! Programming languages only really work due to the syntactical restrictions on how you communicate your ideas. So don't even bother...

But LLMs can be very effective compilers (although they didn't always use to be like that). And we are slowly learning how to leverage English as a programming language. Inherently, we will never actually fully get there, but innovation will get us as close as possible.
